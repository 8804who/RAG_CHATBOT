# 2026 Ballon d'Or

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Ballon_d%27Or
Retrieved: 2026-11-21

## Overview

The 2026 Ballon d'Or was the 70th annual Ballon d'Or ceremony, presented by France Football. It was held on 26 October 2026 at the London Palladium in London. The hosts were Kate Scott and Thierry Henry. The award covered the 2025–26 season, with an eligibility period running from 3 August 2025 to 19 July 2026, the date of the 2026 FIFA World Cup final.

## Men's Ballon d'Or

Harry Kane, the England captain playing for Bayern Munich, won the men's Ballon d'Or with 1,055 points. It was a close vote: Kylian Mbappé finished second with 1,027 points, only 28 points behind. Lamine Yamal was third with 881 points.

The previous year's winner, Ousmane Dembélé, dropped to seventh place.

## Women's Ballon d'Or

The Ballon d'Or Féminin was won by Alexia Putellas of Spain and Barcelona with 618 points, her third Ballon d'Or. Ewa Pajor was second with 532 points and Khadija Shaw third with 344 points.

## Other trophies

- **Men's Kopa Trophy** (best young player): Lamine Yamal (Spain, Barcelona), his third Kopa Trophy.
- **Women's Kopa Trophy**: Vicky López (Spain, Barcelona), her second award.
- **Men's Yashin Trophy** (best goalkeeper): Unai Simón (Spain, Athletic Bilbao), his first award.
- **Women's Yashin Trophy**: Cata Coll (Spain, Barcelona), her first award.
- **Men's Gerd Müller Trophy** (best striker): Harry Kane (England, Bayern Munich), his second Gerd Müller Trophy.
- **Women's Gerd Müller Trophy**: Charlyn Corral (Mexico, Pachuca), her first award.
- **Sócrates Award**: Bukayo Saka (England, Arsenal).
- **Men's Club of the Year**: Paris Saint-Germain was ranked first.

## Summary

Harry Kane became the 2026 Ballon d'Or winner by a 28-point margin over Kylian Mbappé, and also won the Gerd Müller Trophy as the best striker. Spanish players swept several categories: Alexia Putellas won the women's Ballon d'Or, Lamine Yamal and Vicky López won the Kopa Trophies, and Unai Simón and Cata Coll won the Yashin Trophies for goalkeepers. The 2026 ceremony moved from Paris to the London Palladium.
