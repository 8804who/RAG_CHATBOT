# 2026 Winter Olympics (Milano Cortina 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Winter_Olympics, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Winter_Olympics_medal_table
Retrieved: 2026-11-21

## Overview

The 2026 Winter Olympics were held in Italy, hosted jointly by Milan and Cortina d'Ampezzo. The Games officially ran from 6 to 22 February 2026, although some events began on 4 February.

- Nations: 92 (including Individual Neutral Athletes)
- Athletes: 2,884 (1,535 men and 1,349 women)
- Events: 116 events in 8 sports and 16 disciplines

Ski mountaineering made its Olympic debut as an optional sport, with three medal events.

## Ceremonies

The opening ceremony took place on 6 February 2026 at the Stadio San Siro in Milan. The closing ceremony was held on 22 February 2026 at the Verona Arena in Verona. For the first time in the history of the Games, two Olympic cauldrons were lit, one in Milan and one in Cortina d'Ampezzo. The official mascots were Tina and Milo.

## Medal table

A total of 349 medals were awarded: 116 gold, 118 silver and 115 bronze. Twenty-nine National Olympic Committees won at least one medal.

## Records and firsts

- Norway set two Winter Olympic records: its 41 total medals surpassed the previous record of 39 (set in 2018), and its 18 gold medals broke the previous record of 16 (set in 2022).
- Brazil won the first medal and the first gold medal in its Winter Olympic history, becoming the first tropical, Latin American and South American nation to win a Winter Olympic medal.
- Georgia also won the first Winter Olympic medal in its history.
- Host nation Italy finished fourth with 30 medals, including 10 golds; it won more bronze medals (14) than any other nation in the top ten.

## Summary

Milano Cortina 2026 featured 116 events across two main host areas in northern Italy and introduced ski mountaineering. Norway topped the medal table with record totals of 18 gold and 41 medals, ahead of the United States and the Netherlands.
