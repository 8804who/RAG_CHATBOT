# 2025 papal conclave and Pope Leo XIV

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_papal_conclave, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Leo_XIV
Retrieved: 2026-11-21

## Background

Pope Francis died on 21 April 2025. His death led to a papal conclave to elect his successor.

## The conclave

The conclave was held on 7 and 8 May 2025 in the Sistine Chapel in the Apostolic Palace, Vatican City. Of the 135 cardinals eligible to vote, all but two attended, so 133 cardinal electors took part.

Because the Dean of the College of Cardinals, Giovanni Battista Re, was over 80 years old and therefore not eligible to vote, the conclave was presided over by Pietro Parolin, the most senior elector under 80. Re celebrated the opening Mass.

The election was decided on the fourth ballot. White smoke appeared from the Sistine Chapel chimney at 6:07 p.m. on 8 May 2025, followed by the ringing of the bells of St. Peter's Basilica. The protodeacon, Cardinal Dominique Mamberti, then announced the election from the central loggia of St. Peter's Basilica.

## The new pope

The cardinals elected Robert Francis Prevost, an American cardinal, who took the regnal name **Leo XIV**. Before his election he was prefect of the Dicastery for Bishops and president of the Pontifical Commission for Latin America.

### Early life and religious career

Prevost was born on 14 September 1955 at Mercy Hospital in the Bronzeville neighborhood of Chicago, Illinois. He joined the Order of Saint Augustine (the Augustinians) and entered its novitiate on 1 September 1977. He served as Prior Provincial of the Province of Our Mother of Good Counsel from 1998 to 2001, and was then elected Prior General of the Augustinians in 2001, serving two consecutive six-year terms until 2013.

### Peru and the Roman Curia

On 26 September 2015 he was named Bishop of Chiclayo in Peru, a post he held until 2023. In 2015 he became a naturalized Peruvian citizen, as required before his appointment as a diocesan bishop. Pope Francis appointed him prefect of the Dicastery for Bishops on 30 January 2023 and created him a cardinal on 30 September 2023.

### Inauguration

Leo XIV's papal inauguration Mass was held on 18 May 2025 in St. Peter's Square. He was formally installed as Bishop of Rome on 25 May 2025.

## Notable firsts

- First pope from the United States and from North America.
- First pope to hold Peruvian citizenship.
- First pope from the Order of Saint Augustine.
- Second pope from an English-speaking country, after Adrian IV.
- First pope born after World War II.

## Choice of the name Leo

He chose the name Leo to echo Pope Leo XIII, who developed modern Catholic social teaching during the Second Industrial Revolution, with particular concern for workers. The new pope linked the name to the challenges of a new industrial revolution and of artificial intelligence.
