# 2025 Ballon d'Or

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Ballon_d%27Or
Retrieved: 2026-11-21

## Overview

The 2025 Ballon d'Or was the 69th annual Ballon d'Or ceremony, presented by the French magazine France Football. The ceremony took place on 22 September 2025 at the Théâtre du Châtelet in Paris, France. It was hosted by Kate Scott and Ruud Gullit.

## Eligibility period

For the fourth time, the award was based on the football season rather than the calendar year. The men's award covered performances from 1 August 2024 to 13 July 2025, a window that ended with the final of the 2025 FIFA Club World Cup. The women's award covered the period up to 2 August 2025, ending with the final of the 2025 Copa América Femenina.

## Men's Ballon d'Or

The men's Ballon d'Or was won by Ousmane Dembélé, the French forward of Paris Saint-Germain. He received 1,380 points, and it was the first Ballon d'Or of his career. Barcelona's Lamine Yamal finished second with 1,059 points, and Dembélé's club team-mate Vitinha was third with 703 points.

## Women's Ballon d'Or

The Ballon d'Or Féminin was won by Aitana Bonmatí of Spain and Barcelona with 506 points. It was her third Ballon d'Or. The podium was completed by two Arsenal players: Mariona Caldentey, second with 478 points, and Alessia Russo, third with 420 points.

## Other trophies

- **Men's Kopa Trophy** (best young player): Lamine Yamal (Spain) with 124 points, his second Kopa Trophy. Désiré Doué was second with 72 points.
- **Women's Kopa Trophy**: Vicky López (Spain), her first award.
- **Men's Yashin Trophy** (best goalkeeper): Gianluigi Donnarumma (Italy), his second award.
- **Women's Yashin Trophy**: Hannah Hampton (England), her first award.
- **Men's Gerd Müller Trophy** (best striker): Viktor Gyökeres (Sweden), his first award.
- **Women's Gerd Müller Trophy**: Ewa Pajor (Poland), her first award.

## Summary

The 2025 edition was dominated by Paris Saint-Germain on the men's side, with Dembélé winning the main award and Donnarumma taking the goalkeeper trophy, while Spain's Aitana Bonmatí won the women's award for the third time. Lamine Yamal, runner-up in the main ranking, retained the Kopa Trophy for the best young player.
